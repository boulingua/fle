## Synthetic voice — ATTRIBUTION REQUIRED

Audio in this course is synthesised with the Piper voice `fr_FR-mls-medium`, trained from
scratch on **Multilingual LibriSpeech (MLS)**, <http://openslr.org/94/>, released under
**CC BY 4.0**.

CC BY 4.0 requires attribution, and this file is where that obligation is met.
It is not a formality: the corpus is the reason this course can publish audio
at all, after the voice it previously used was found to descend from a
NonCommercial base model.

MLS is derived from LibriVox public-domain audiobook recordings.
